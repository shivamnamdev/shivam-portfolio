# PRD 21: The learner portal reads the database (phase 5)

Status: slices 5a, 5b, 5c and 5d built 2026-11-06, slice 5e (admin learner preview) built 2026-11-07. Blueprint (approved): [docs/architecture/admin-portal-phase5-learner-portal.md](../../architecture/admin-portal-phase5-learner-portal.md). Later slices (5b learning content and private links, 5c banners, bell and templates, 5d prices and coupons) extend this PRD.

## 1. Flags
Four server environment variables (never `NEXT_PUBLIC_`), each `files` (the default) or `database`: `PORTAL_CATALOG`, `PORTAL_LEARNING`, `PORTAL_ENGAGEMENT`, `PORTAL_COUPONS`. Only the exact word `database` (any case) turns one on; any other value, a typo or an empty value means `files`. A flag set to `database` that cannot read its rows (error) or finds nothing published falls back to the data files **for that request**, logs one line without secrets, and never shows an error page. Turning a flag back to `files` is the rollback; nothing in the database changes. A change of a flag needs a restart or redeploy (it is read from the environment).

## 2. Slice 5a: the course list and sales pages (`PORTAL_CATALOG`)
Code: `lib/catalog/{source,tags,mapping}.ts` (pure, unit-tested) and `lib/catalog/catalog.ts` (server only); pages `app/courses/page.tsx` and `app/courses/[slug]/page.tsx`; `WhatsAppWidget` takes the support number as a prop. No SQL, no new package.

**Rules under test:**
* **Source:** with `files` the pages answer from `data/courses.ts` exactly as before and the database is never read. With `database` the list and pages are built from the **published** courses and cohorts (a cohort whose course is not published is not shown; drafts and archived items never appear). The QA fixture course (test environments only) stays available whichever source is on.
* **What is built:** the cohort gives identity, texts, status, price and the open or closed flag; the course's `pitch` gives only the known sales blocks (`vsl`, `painPoints`, `resources`, `differentiators`, `cohortHighlights`, `courseTestimonials`, `outcomes`, `bonuses`, `modules`). **A pitch can never set a slug, id, price, status, `listed` flag or link.** A block left out of the pitch stays absent (each sales component already hides or defaults a missing block).
* **Private links:** the live and WhatsApp links of a portal cohort are never read into the catalog: they are not in the page, the props or the HTML (slice 5b adds the access-checked route). After a purchase or free enrolment the success box takes the WhatsApp link from the server's reply (`verify-payment` already returned it; `enroll-free` now returns it too).
* **`listed`:** a cohort with `listed: false` is left out of `/courses` but its direct address still works (as today).
* **Price and open or closed follow the payment source** (the invariant "the amount shown equals the amount charged"): until `PORTAL_COUPONS` is `database` (slice 5d), orders are still priced from the data files, so a cohort that exists in the files shows the **file's** price and open flag, and a cohort that exists **only in the portal shows as closed** (the server could not charge it). When `PORTAL_COUPONS` is `database`, the portal's numbers and open flag are used. Prices are written like the site writes them (`₹1,999`, `$9.99`), a savings line only when there is a real saving, and a plans cohort shows its lowest plan until checkout learns plans (5d).
* **Support number:** the "chat with us" button uses the `support-phone` setting (digits only, India's 91 added to a plain 10-digit number; a number starting with 0 or an unusable one is ignored) and the long-standing default otherwise.
* **Freshness:** a saved change to a course, cohort or setting refreshes the cached list and pages at once (`revalidateTag('catalog')` after the audit entry is written); a 60-second timer is only a backstop.

**Unit (`tests/unit/catalog.spec.ts`):** `CAT-UNIT-SRC-001`, `002`, `CAT-UNIT-TAG-001`, `CAT-UNIT-MAP-001` to `006`, and the drift guard `CAT-UNIT-DRIFT-001` (what the portal would build from every real course in the data files equals the files: sales blocks, texts and price strings; links not carried).
**UI (`tests/ui/catalog/portal-catalog.spec.ts`, needs the server and the test run started with `PORTAL_CATALOG=database`, for example `PORT=3100 PORTAL_CATALOG=database E2E_SEED_PERSONAS=1 npx playwright test tests/ui/catalog`):** `CAT-UI-001` to `006` (empty portal falls back to the files; published items show, draft and unlisted do not; no private link in the HTML; portal-only cohort shows closed; change and setting show at once; unpublishing returns the files). Fixtures are `qa-cat-*`, removed afterwards; the support-phone setting is saved and restored.
**Matrix:** the existing public, discovery, sales-funnel and smoke suites are run with the flag `files` and again with `database` (nothing published, so the fallback answers): both must pass unchanged.
**Not automated:** the visual look of a page built from the portal; the production build's static generation (the list page is prerendered at build time and refreshed by the tag); a failure of the database in the middle of a request.

## 3. Slice 5b: lessons, the dashboard cards and the private links (`PORTAL_LEARNING`)
Code: `lib/catalog/learning.ts` (server only, with the pure `lessons.ts`), three learner routes `GET /api/learning/content/{slug}`, `/links/{slug}` and `/my-courses`; the lesson page, live lobby, dashboard, public share page and the certificate rule read through them. No SQL, no new package. The learner slug check now accepts `_` as the admin portal does.

**Rules under test:**
* **Source:** with `files` the lessons, titles and links come from `data/learning-content.ts` and `data/courses.ts` as before. With `database` they come from the cohort's **published** curriculum (never the draft) and its cohort and course documents (any status, so a finished cohort stays open to the people enrolled in it); a cohort with nothing published, or a database error, falls back to the files for that request.
* **Access check on the server:** `content` and `links` answer 200 only to a learner enrolled in that cohort or the admin, **403** to any other signed-in learner, **401** to a visitor, and **400** to a malformed slug; the answer to a refused caller holds none of the data. Nothing here depends on the page's own check any more.
* **Nothing private in the browser:** the lesson list, the lab, solution and expected-outcome links, and the live and WhatsApp links are no longer bundled with any page (the old pages imported both data files for every visitor). The sales page and the dashboard get no link in their props (in either source); the lesson page learns only **whether** a live class exists; the live lobby fetches the live link after the access check; the success box after a purchase or free enrolment uses the link in the server's reply. `/links` is never cached (`private, no-store`).
* **Dashboard:** `my-courses` returns the cards (id, slug, title, status text, duration, syllabus) of the learner's own enrolments in catalog order, no link; none held gives an empty list.
* **Share page and certificate:** the public share page names the lesson (the assignment title, or "Lesson n") and the course from the same source; the certificate percentage and course title use the same lessons as the lesson page.
* **Freshness:** a saved change to a curriculum, cohort or course refreshes the cached lessons, titles and cards at once (`revalidateTag('learning')`); links are read fresh.

**Unit (`tests/unit/catalog.spec.ts`):** `CAT-UNIT-MAP-007` (no private link in a page object), `CAT-UNIT-LES-001` (the lesson lookup equals the original code for every lesson in the files).
**API on the default server (`tests/api/learning-content.spec.ts`):** `LRN5B-001` to `006`. **With `PORTAL_LEARNING=database` (`tests/api/learning-content-db.spec.ts`, started as `PORT=3100 PORTAL_LEARNING=database E2E_SEED_PERSONAS=1 npx playwright test tests/api/learning-content-db.spec.ts`):** `LRN5B-DB-001` to `004` (portal lessons, title and live flag; links only to the enrolled learner; a publish and a link change show at once, an unpublished edit does not; the share page).
**Bundle (`tests/ui/learning/bundle-secrets.spec.ts`):** `LRN5B-BND-001` and `002`: the JavaScript files and the HTML the server sends for the sales pages, dashboard, lesson page and live lobby contain none of the private links, the first lesson id or the lab repository; the test also checks that enough JavaScript (including the lesson page chunk) was scanned.
**Matrix:** the dashboard, learning, learner-API and exam specs are run on the fixture server with all flags `files` and again with `database` (nothing published, so the fallback answers).
**Not automated:** how the lesson page looks for a portal-only cohort in the browser (the data path is covered by the API spec); the lesson page when the content route fails mid-session.

## 4. Slice 5c: banners, the notification bell and e-mail templates (`PORTAL_ENGAGEMENT`)
Code: `lib/catalog/{bell,mail,bannerView}.ts` (pure, unit-tested) and `lib/catalog/engagement.ts` (server only); routes `GET /api/banners?placement=` (public), `GET /api/learning/notifications` and `POST /api/learning/notifications/read`; components `Banners` and `NotificationBell` (the bell moved out of `Navbar`); the paid-enrolment, free-enrolment and sign-up mails. No SQL (the notification tables already exist from step 8), no new package. `UrgencyBanner` was unused and is not touched. Owner decision (2026-11-06): **a mail uses a template only when one is saved**.

**Rules under test:**
* **Source:** with `files` (default) no banner is served anywhere, the bell is the old hand-written list (source `"files"`, the browser keeps the read count as before) and **every mail is exactly today's**. With `database`, banners and the bell come from the portal and a mail may use a saved template. Any database failure answers like "nothing published": no banner, an empty bell, today's mail.
* **Banners:** one rule decides what a viewer sees, the existing `visibleBanners` (published, inside its window, aimed at the viewer, best priority first; the top banner shows alone unless it allows several). The browser asks `/api/banners?placement=` for one of `site-top`, `courses`, `course-page`, `learning`, `dashboard` (anything else is 400) and receives only slug, version, message, button, style, dismissibility, seconds left and priority, **never the audience**. A visitor gets the visitor and everyone banners; a signed-in learner also gets those for learners, their cohorts and their plans. Not cached (`private, no-store`); the server's banner list is cached with a tag and refreshed at once by any banner change.
* **Banner bar:** plain text only (React escapes it); the countdown ticks every second from the seconds the server sent (`HH:MM:SS`, with days from one day up) and disappears when it runs out; **dismiss** is remembered per banner **version** in the browser, so an edited banner shows again; a banner marked not dismissible has no close button. The `site-top` banner sits above the navigation on every page; the others sit on the courses page, a sales page, the lesson page and the dashboard.
* **Bell:** a learner sees live notifications (published, not expired, never an admin alert) aimed at everyone, their cohort, their plan or themselves, newest first (at most 30), each with its own read state; the body is shown as plain text. Opening an item marks it read; "Mark all as read" marks everything in their bell. Read state is per learner and kept on the server; an id the learner cannot see, or an unknown one, is ignored and writes nothing; bad ids are 400; a visitor is 401. Deleting a notification removes it from the bell at once.
* **Mails (only when a template is saved):** `payment-receipt` replaces the paid-enrolment mail, `welcome` the sign-up mail, and `free-enrolment` **adds** a mail on free enrolment (which sends none today). The text is rendered by the same renderer as the preview (everything escaped, only the template's own placeholders), the subject is one line, and a saved template that no longer passes the rules is ignored, so today's mail goes out. A mail failure never undoes an enrolment or a sign-up.

**Unit (`tests/unit/portal-engagement.spec.ts`):** `ENG-UNIT-BELL-001` to `004`, `ENG-UNIT-MAIL-001`, `002`, `ENG-UNIT-BAN-001`, `ENG-UNIT-TAG-001`.
**API:** default server `tests/api/portal-engagement-files.spec.ts` (`ENG-FILES-001`, `002`); with the flag `tests/api/portal-engagement-db.spec.ts` (`ENG-DB-001` to `005`; start as `PORT=3100 PORTAL_ENGAGEMENT=database E2E_SEED_PERSONAS=1 npx playwright test tests/api/portal-engagement-db.spec.ts`).
**UI with the flag (`tests/ui/portal/engagement.spec.ts`):** `ENG-UI-001` to `004` (countdown ticks, link, dismiss and its memory, a new version shows again; site-top above the navigation; dashboard and cohort banners by audience; the bell with dot, count, plain text, per-item and all-read, persistence after reload, a new item brings the dot back).
**Matrix:** the public, discovery, dashboard and learning suites are run with all flags `files` and again with `database`.
**Not automated:** a real e-mail send with a saved template (no test sends mail; the rendering and the decision are unit-tested, the three send sites are one-line changes), and the look of the bar on a phone.


## 5. Slice 5d: prices and coupons from the portal (`PORTAL_COUPONS`)

Built on `integrate/course-cta-update-3` (plan-based pricing, `planId` in the order notes) merged with the phase 4 and 5 work; slice 5d needs that code, so **`course-cta-update-3` must reach `main` before this slice does**.

* **Source:** `PORTAL_COUPONS=files` (default) keeps `data/courses.ts` and `data/coupons.ts` exactly as before. **Prices and coupons come from the portal only when both `PORTAL_CATALOG` and `PORTAL_COUPONS` are `database`** (`pricingSource`): the page shows the data files whenever the catalog flag is off, so the server must charge what the page shows. Reading goes through `lib/catalog/commerce.ts`, used by `create-order`, `enroll-free`, `verify-payment` and the new `POST /api/coupons/check`.
* **What is sold:** a **published** cohort of a **published** course, priced from its numbers (single INR and optional USD, or plans in INR). A cohort that is draft, archived, unknown, or whose course is not published answers "Course not found"; a closed cohort "Enrollment is closed"; USD on a cohort without a USD price, or on a plans cohort, "This course is priced in INR only"; a plans cohort needs a valid `planId`. If the portal has **no** published cohort at all, or cannot be read, the request is answered from the files (as the catalog does). The QA fixture course stays available from the files.
* **Plan cards:** the sales page shows the plans that price the order (the cohort's plans in database mode, the file's plans in files mode); the course card shows the lowest plan price.
* **Coupons (database mode):** the code is looked up in the `coupons` table (codes are case-insensitive); the old four file codes **stop working** (owner decision) and only the environment-driven `QA100` is still honoured. One pure function (`lib/catalog/couponRules.ts`) decides, in this order: unknown or switched off = 400 "Invalid coupon code"; a used one-time code = 400 "This coupon has already been used."; expired = 400 "This coupon has expired."; a selected code for another account = **403** "This coupon is not valid for your account."; wrong cohort = 400 "This coupon does not apply to this course."; wrong plan = 400 "This coupon does not apply to this plan."; open or selected code at its limit = 400 "This coupon has been fully used."; a one-time code held by someone else = 400 "This coupon is being used right now. Try again in a few minutes."; a result between zero and the payment minimum = 400 "This coupon cannot be applied to this price." A coupon table that cannot be read refuses with **503 "Could not check the coupon. Please try again."** (never the old codes).
* **One-time codes:** `create-order` and `enroll-free` **hold** the code for the buyer for 30 minutes (one conditional update, so two buyers cannot both win); `verify-payment` or the free enrolment **spends** it; an order that cannot be created, or a free request that finds the learner already enrolled, **releases** it; an abandoned order frees itself when the 30 minutes pass. A retried verification (the learner is already enrolled) spends nothing twice. Open and selected codes with a limit are **counted at payment verification or free enrolment**; because the count is taken then, a burst of orders opened before any is paid can pass the limit by the number of open orders (documented limitation, owner informed).
* **Browser:** with the coupon source on `database` the pages pass `couponCheck="server"`: the coupon box calls `POST /api/coupons/check` (signed-in only; `{ success, coupon }` in the shape checkout already uses, or the refusal text and status above), so **the codes are not in the page**. In a plans cohort one coupon box serves all plan cards: the code is accepted if any plan takes it and `create-order` re-checks the chosen plan. In files mode the browser still checks the file codes. The file codes remain in the client JavaScript until slice 6e removes that source (SCP-53 closes then).
* **Success screens** now use the WhatsApp link the server returns (`enroll-free` and `verify-payment` answer with it; the page no longer carries it).
* **Also fixed here:** a portal course with no sales pitch no longer crashes its page (the closing heading falls back to a plain one).

**Unit (`tests/unit/portal-coupons.spec.ts`):** `PCP-UNIT-001` to `012` (every refusal and its text/status, per-currency discounts, plan and cohort scope, limits, one-time states, the payment minimum, `pricingSource`, plan cards and plan pricing, INR-only, the page object's plans in both sources).
**API (`tests/api/portal-coupons-db.spec.ts`, start as `PORT=3100 PORTAL_CATALOG=database PORTAL_COUPONS=database E2E_SEED_PERSONAS=1 npx playwright test tests/api/portal-coupons-db.spec.ts`):** `PCP-DB-001` to `008` (set-up; order amounts per plan and the refusals; check route; selected and scope; counted limit; one-time use and release; hold, busy, pay and retry, using Razorpay test keys; `QA100`).
**UI (`tests/ui/portal/coupons.spec.ts`, same flags):** `PCP-UI-001` (plan cards, no code in the page HTML, wrong code text, server-applied percentage), `PCP-UI-002` (course list).
**Matrix:** payment, enrolment and learner API specs and the discovery, public, sales-funnel and smoke UI specs run with all flags `files` and again with all four `database`.
**Not automated:** a real card payment (test keys only; `verify-payment` is driven with a signed test payment), the owner recreating coupons before turning the flag on, the look of the plan cards on a phone.

## 6. Slice 5e: the admin-only learner preview (`/preview/courses`)

Blueprint (approved 2026-11-07): [docs/architecture/admin-portal-draft-preview.md](../../architecture/admin-portal-draft-preview.md). Drafts never appear on the public pages; the admin sees them, as a learner will, on two separate admin-only routes.

* **Access:** `app/preview/layout.tsx` runs the admin check on the server before anything is read: a visitor goes to the home page, a signed-in learner to `/learning`; `robots: noindex`, `force-dynamic`, not in the middleware's public list. No draft text is ever in a non-admin response.
* **List (`/preview/courses`):** the public catalog's own card component (`components/catalog/CourseGrid.tsx`, shared with `/courses`) for every cohort in the portal, each card tagged **draft**, **published** or **archived** (plus a note when its course is missing, draft or archived). Draft and published show by default (drafts first); the **Show** dropdown (`?show=archived`) lists only archived cohorts, greyed out. Prices are the portal's (a plans cohort shows its lowest plan, as on the public list).
* **Page (`/preview/courses/{cohort}`):** the public sales page itself (`components/catalog/CourseSalesView.tsx`, shared with `/courses/{slug}`) with one slim label (draft, published or archived) and the checkout section **inert** (no coupon box or Buy button works) and the mobile sticky bar removed. The order routes also refuse a draft with "Course not found", so nothing can be bought from a preview. Banners are not shown in a preview.
* **Data:** `lib/catalog/preview.ts` reads `content_docs` (course and cohort rows, any status), never cached, **independent of the `PORTAL_*` flags**, never reads the `private` part of a cohort (no links). Pure rules (mode, order, notes) are in `lib/catalog/previewView.ts`.
* **Entry points:** "Preview as learner" on the Courses screen, the Cohorts screen and each cohort, and a "Learner preview" entry in the admin sidebar (opens in a new tab).
* **Public pages unchanged:** the two public routes now render the same shared components; their output is the same (the discovery, public, sales-funnel and smoke suites pass with the flags on `files` and `database`).

**Unit (`tests/unit/portal-preview.spec.ts`):** `PRV-UNIT-001` to `006` (mode parsing, order, course notes, statuses, no private link in a preview object, the sidebar entry).
**UI (`tests/ui/portal/preview.spec.ts`, any flags):** `PRV-UI-001` set-up; `002` a visitor is refused and gets no draft text; `003` a learner is sent to `/learning`; `004` the list shows draft and published with their tags, hides archived, "Archived only" shows just archived greyed; `005` a draft opens as the real page with the label and plan cards, checkout inert, refused by the order route, absent from the public pages; `006` a published cohort is read-only, a missing page is 404, an unpublished course is noted on the cards; `007` publishing makes it public (database catalog) and the label changes, and the admin screens link to the preview.
**Not automated:** the look of the label and greyed cards on a phone.
